About Us

Bridge Hallmark School is located at Wellington Crescent, Mosogar, Delta State. We are a premier institution offering holistic education from pre-nursery to secondary level. Our motto is "Education for the development of the general human being".

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in education, producing well-rounded individuals who will make a positive impact in society.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops the intellectual, social, and moral capabilities of every student.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Discipline, Respect, and Service to humanity.
